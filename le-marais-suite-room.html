Le Marais Suite

Suite · Clarence House · Ground Floor
Clarence House

Beautifully composed.
Made for longer stays.

Le Marais Suite is one of two ground-floor suites at Clarence House — a double bedroom with its own separate lounge, an original fireplace that makes the sitting room feel genuinely lived in, and a freestanding bathtub in the en-suite. The building was once a family home to the aristocracy of Alderney, and that sense of settled comfort is still very much present. The kind of room you book for a weekend and leave wishing you'd stayed longer.
