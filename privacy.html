Privacy Policy.

The Blonde Hedgehog Limited · Le Huret, Saint Anne, Alderney GY9 3UY · Last updated October 2025

What We Collect

When you book, contact us, or sign up for communications, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, postal address, payment details, dietary requirements, and any preferences relevant to your stay. We use cookies on this website for basic analytics purposes.

How We Use It

We use your information to manage your booking and stay, communicate with you, process payments, and send occasional marketing communications where you have consented. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, or to unsubscribe from marketing communications, please contact us at reservations@blondehedgehog.com.

Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Payment card data is processed by a PCI-compliant payment processor and is never stored on our systems.

Contact

For any questions relating to this policy, please contact The Blonde Hedgehog Limited at reservations@blondehedgehog.com or call +44 (0)1481 823230.
